Manage Me

A private personal finance and tax organizer that runs on your own computer.

What it does

Manage Me keeps one household's finances in one place. It brings in bank and card transactions, sorts them into categories, tracks balances and transfers between accounts, and gathers tax documents so tax time is easier.

The app runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a hosted service, and there are no public sign-ups.

Data & Privacy

What bank data we access

Manage Me uses Plaid (Transactions product only) to import your accounts (name, type, last 4 digits, and balance) and their transactions. It does not request full account or routing numbers, identity details, or the ability to move money. You sign in to your bank through Plaid. Manage Me never sees or stores your bank password.

Where it's stored

All data is stored in a local SQLite database on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored in the cloud.

How bank connections are protected

Plaid access tokens are encrypted at rest. When you disconnect an account, its access is revoked through Plaid and the stored token is deleted. You can disconnect at any time from inside the app.

Optional AI helper

Manage Me has an optional AI helper that suggests categories. It is off by default. If you turn it on, redacted payee text, amounts, and account type are sent to OpenAI for category suggestions. No account numbers, credentials, or tokens are sent.

How it's used

Your data is used only to power the app. It is never sold or shared for advertising.
