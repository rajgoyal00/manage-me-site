Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Effective 2026-09-28 · Last reviewed 2026-09-28

What is kept

Accounts (name, type, last 4 digits, balance), transactions, and card and loan details imported through Plaid are stored in a local SQLite database on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored in the cloud.

How long it is kept

Data is kept while the bank account is connected, so the app can show history and tax summaries.

Disconnecting revokes access at Plaid and deletes the stored access token. It does not delete transactions already imported; they stay in the local database unless you tick 'Also remove the transactions' when disconnecting, or delete them later. To request deletion, email rajgoyal00@gmail.com.

Consent records

A record of each connection's consent is kept after disconnecting: when it was connected, disconnected or revoked, which Plaid products were used, and Plaid's internal account IDs. It contains no transactions, balances or account numbers.

Backups

Copies of the database are made before app updates and kept on the same encrypted computer. Backups older than 90 days are deleted during the quarterly review. When a deletion request is completed, backups that still hold that data are deleted too.

Requesting deletion

Email rajgoyal00@gmail.com with what you want deleted. Requests are completed within 30 days, and you'll get a reply when they're done. You can also revoke Manage Me's access at any time through Plaid Portal.
