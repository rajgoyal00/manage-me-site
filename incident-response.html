Incident Response Plan

Effective 2026-09-28 · Last reviewed 2026-09-28

What counts as an incident

Any sign that bank data, a Plaid access token, or a key or password might have been seen or used by someone other than the owner. Examples: a lost or stolen computer, a leaked API key, an unexpected sign-in to the Plaid Dashboard, GitHub or OpenAI, or malware on the computer.

Steps

  1. Contain. Revoke the affected Plaid connections, from the app or the Plaid Dashboard. If the computer holding the database is lost, revoke all of them.
  2. Rotate. Rotate the Plaid secret and the OpenAI key. Rotate the app's encryption key and session signing key (this ends every session), and reset its two-step sign-in. Change passwords on any affected account.
  3. Investigate. Work out what was exposed and how, using the app's logs, its consent log, and the account activity pages of Plaid, GitHub and OpenAI.
  4. Notify. Promptly email anyone whose data was affected, and notify Plaid.
  5. Fix and review. Fix the cause, then write down what happened and what changed.

Review

This plan is reviewed at least once a year and after any incident.
